Protein Concentration Calculator

Manual entry or Excel template upload for Bradford, BCA, and ELISA-style protein concentration calculations. Replicate absorbance values can be separated by comma, semicolon, space, or entered in Excel replicate columns.

1. Standard curve

Enter standard concentration and raw absorbance/OD values. Corrected absorbance = mean raw absorbance − standard blank.
